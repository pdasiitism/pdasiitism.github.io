Prabal Das, Ph.D.

I am a hydro-meteorology and climate science researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, Bayesian networks, numerical weather prediction, and hydrologic risk. My work focuses on improving rainfall, streamflow, drought, climate-extreme, and winter precipitation-type prediction using explainable and operationally useful data-driven methods.

I currently serve as Lead Researcher for Human Centered Weather Forecasting at the Development Innovation Lab - India, University of Chicago Trust. Previously, I worked as a Post-Doctoral Research Associate and Adjunct Professor at The University of Texas at Arlington, following my Ph.D. in Engineering from IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.
